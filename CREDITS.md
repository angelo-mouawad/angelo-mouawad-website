## Made in code

The desk, monitor, mini fridge, mug, game cases, orb lamp, shelves, window, blinds, Bible and its stand, poster, TV, corner light, rug pattern and the city outside the window are built in code and have no external source.

## Not carried over from the old site

- `banana_plant.glb` is under the Sketchfab Standard licence, not a Creative Commons one, so it was left out.
- `desk_lamp.glb`, `plant.glb`, `ceiling_light_round.glb` and `low_poly_cartoon_football_ball_free.glb` were replaced by Poly Haven models.
- `gaming_pc.glb` and `mini_fridge.glb` were replaced by versions built in code.
- `gaming_chair.glb`, `gaming_mouse.glb`, the Poly Haven `gamepad.glb`, the coded keyboard and the Poly Pizza PC were later replaced by the models above. A Fractal Meshify C case and a MacBook Pro 2021 model (CC BY-NC) were tried and dropped.
